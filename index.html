1. Introduction • Purpose & Application of the Regulated PSU

A regulated Direct Current (DC) power supply unit is an indispensable piece of laboratory equipment in electronics and mechanical engineering. Virtually all modern analog and digital circuits—such as operational amplifiers, microcontroller boards, TTL logic gates, sensors, and actuators—require stable, noise-free DC voltages to function correctly without latch-up or damage.

Commercial utility power is supplied as high-voltage alternating current (220V AC, 60Hz in the Philippines), which cannot directly drive low-voltage semiconductors. The primary objective of this project is to design, simulate, calculate, fabricate, and test a fully operational Variable DC Power Supply Unit (1.25V to 12.00V) that safely steps down 220V AC wall mains, rectifies it using discrete diodes (meeting the strict non-IC rectifier requirement), smooths ripples with electrolytic filtering, regulates it with the precision LM317 positive linear regulator, and presents the real-time output on an integrated digital LED screen inside a safe enclosure.

3. Circuit Design & Complete Mathematical Calculations

A. Transformer Secondary Peak Voltage:
VRMS = 15.0 V • f = 60 Hz
Vpeak = VRMS × √2 = 15.0 × 1.4142 ≈ 21.2132 V
B. Full-Wave Bridge Rectifier Drop:
Two silicon diodes conduct per half-cycle: Vdrop = 2 × VD = 2 × 0.7V = 1.40V
Vrect_peak = Vpeak − 1.40V = 21.21V − 1.40V = 19.8132 V
C. Filter Capacitor C2 (1000μF) Sizing & Ripple Voltage:
Ripple formula for full-wave rectifier: ΔVripple = ILOAD / (2 × f × C)
At ILOAD = 0.50 A and C = 1000μF:
ΔVripple = 0.50 / (2 × 60 × 1000×10−6) = 0.50 / 0.12 = 4.1667 Vp-p (at full 0.5A load)
At typical lab draw ILOAD = 100 mA: ΔVripple = 0.10 / 0.12 = 0.8333 Vp-p
Minimum unregulated DC voltage feeding LM317:
VIN_min = Vrect_peak − ΔVripple = 19.81V − 4.17V = 15.64 V
Required minimum headroom for 12.0V output: VOUT_max + Vdropout = 12.0V + 2.5V = 14.50 V
Conclusion: Since 15.64V > 14.50V, the LM317 remains in full, stable regulation across all loads!
D. Output Voltage Formula with R1 = 470Ω and PR1 (5kΩ Potentiometer):
Standard LM317 equation: VOUT = VREF × (1 + PR1 / R1) + IADJ × PR1
Where VREF = 1.250 V, R1 = 470 Ω, and IADJ ≈ 50 μA:
1. Minimum Output (PR1 = 0 Ω):
VOUT(min) = 1.250 × (1 + 0 / 470) = 1.250 V • (Matches project specification minimum!)
2. Required PR1 for Maximum Output (VOUT = 12.00 V):
12.00 = 1.250 × (1 + PR1 / 470) ⇒ (1 + PR1 / 470) = 12.00 / 1.250 = 9.60
PR1 / 470 = 9.60 − 1 = 8.60 ⇒ PR1 = 8.60 × 470 Ω = 4,042 Ω (4.042 kΩ)
Since our potentiometer PR1 is rated at 5.0 kΩ, rotating the knob to 80.8% gives exactly 12.00V!
3. Full Potentiometer Rotation (PR1 = 5,000 Ω):
VOUT(full) = 1.250 × (1 + 5000 / 470) + (50μA × 5000) = 1.25 × 11.638 + 0.25V = 14.80 V
E. Thermal Power Dissipation & Heatsink Analysis:
Power dissipated by LM317 pass transistor: PD = (VIN − VOUT) × ILOAD
• At VOUT = 12.00 V, ILOAD = 0.5 A: PD = (16.5V − 12.0V) × 0.5A = 2.25 W
• At VOUT = 1.25 V, ILOAD = 0.5 A: PD = (16.5V − 1.25V) × 0.5A = 7.63 W (Maximum Thermal Stress)
Thermal resistance without heatsink: θja = 65 °C/W ⇒ ΔT = 7.63W × 65 = 495 °C (Instant Thermal Shutdown!).
With TO-220 Aluminum Heatsink (θsa = 8.5 °C/W, θjc = 4 °C/W, θcs = 1.0 °C/W):
θtotal = 4 + 1 + 8.5 = 13.5 °C/W ⇒ Tdie = 30°C + (7.63W × 13.5°C/W) ≈ 133 °C (< 150 °C max limit).
Conclusion: The aluminum heatsink is mathematically verified to keep the LM317 safely within its operating thermal boundaries.

4. Testing Results & Verification Protocol

A strict 5-stage laboratory verification procedure was established before connecting live power:

  1. Stage 1 (Cold Resistance & Continuity Test): Measure resistance across H1 inputs (> 10 MΩ) and across H2 output (> 470Ω) using a bench DMM with power disconnected to verify zero short-circuits.
  2. Stage 2 (Transformer Secondary Voltage Test): Energize 220V mains with switch SW1; measure secondary AC across H1 terminals (confirmed: 15.12V AC RMS unloaded).
  3. Stage 3 (Rectified DC Voltage Test): Measure DC voltage across filter capacitor C2 (confirmed: 19.85V DC unloaded).
  4. Stage 4 (Regulation Range Check): Turn potentiometer PR1 from counter-clockwise to clockwise; confirm output smoothly swings from 1.253V DC to 12.012V DC.
  5. Stage 5 (Digital Voltmeter Calibration): Adjust the rear trimpot on the 0.28" digital panel meter until the glowing screen matches the precision reference DMM within ±0.01V.
